TASK
Build a 4x4 sprite sheet (16 cells, read left to right, top to bottom) showing ONE unbroken
character action. Everything is DRAWN FROM SCRATCH - there is no reference image, and nothing
here is a modification of an existing picture.
- STYLE: <<< the art direction, as a look a renderer can hit: "high-fidelity 3D game render,
  cinematic Chinese-fantasy art direction, PBR materials, warm key light and soft rim light" /
  "flat cel-shaded mobile-game chibi" / "16-bit pixel-art game sprite" >>>
- CHARACTER: <<< one short line naming the single figure, e.g. "one figure only - a monk with a
  ringed staff" >>>
- ACTION: <<< what that figure does across the 16 cells, in one sentence >>>

CHARACTER - the one and only figure (named before anything else)
<<< 3-5 lines locked down the way a character sheet does it. Cover, in this order:
    1. who/what it is, age, build, face, and every worn item (headwear, garment, footwear,
       ornament) with its colour and material - name the pieces, do not say "traditional
       clothes";
    2. which way it faces and from what angle it is shot (e.g. "faces to the RIGHT, seen in
       three-quarter front view from slightly below eye level");
    3. any prop it carries, described as a RIGID object with an exact count of its distinctive
       features ("a staff whose finial carries exactly nine rings") and a list of what the prop
       never does (never changes length, never bends, never loses a ring, never swapped for
       another weapon);
    4. the population line: "Exactly two arms, two hands and one <prop>. No helper, no monster,
       no second figure, no animal, no audience - nothing else in the picture is alive." >>>

LOOK
- Background: <<< a flat, seamless, evenly lit <colour> field. No scenery, no props, no horizon
  line, no banding, no texture. >>> For a game asset a single flat colour is the safest choice;
  a busy environment is where identity drift and jitter come from.
- A soft contact shadow sits on the ground under the feet and stays grounded.
- The only glow is <<< the character's own effect and its fixed hue >>>. It never becomes fire,
  lightning, smoke or a rainbow, and its hue stays the same in every cell.
- Identical camera angle, background, lighting and white balance in all 16 cells.

THE ACTION - one continuous motion, 16 equal samples
- The 16 cells are 16 equal-time samples of ONE single unbroken motion. Every cell is a
  different instant of the same action: no cell repeats another, no pose is held across two
  cells, the motion never reverses, and the amount of change between neighbouring cells stays
  as even as the action physically allows.
- The motion: <<< one sentence, start pose -> the main beat -> the recovery. Describe it as ONE
  arc, never as "cells 1-4 do A, cells 5-9 do B" - staged lists make the model jump at the
  phase boundary (hard rule 5). >>>
- ANCHORS (milestones, not stages - each one is just a cell you can describe in words):
  - Cell 1: <<< the opening pose, e.g. "ready stance, <prop> planted upright beside him, no
    glow, both feet on the ground" >>>
  - Cell 8: <<< roughly the midpoint, e.g. "<prop> head lifted high beside the shoulder, the
    light forming at the finial, robe hem starting to lift" >>>
  - Cell 16: <<< the closing pose. THIS IS THE LOOP SEAM: end it almost back at the cell-1
    stance with the effect faded to an afterglow, so a plain cycle loop reads seamless. >>>
- Only the effect may grow: <<< how it appears, swells, and fades >>>. Its shape may change
  freely; nothing else in the picture may.

CAMERA - LOCKED
- The camera does NOT move at all: no orbit, no turn, no zoom, no dolly, no pan, no tilt, no
  roll. Fixed distance, fixed height, fixed focal length, fixed background - the same framing
  in all 16 cells.
- The character stays in the middle of the frame at the SAME scale and the same distance from
  the camera in every cell. It is never re-framed, never slides sideways across the frame, and
  never grows or shrinks.
<<< Note for the operator: this is the exact opposite of the orbit60 templates. There the
camera travels and the background slides (hard rule 17); here the camera is nailed down and the
character does all the moving. Do not copy the "background must slide" language into this file.
>>>
- The camera is not circling the character and the character is not rotating to face the camera.
  The only things moving are the character and the effect it releases.

WHAT MUST NOT CHANGE
- Face, headwear, garment pattern and colours, ornaments, prop, body proportions and identity
  are identical in all 16 cells. The character never changes costume and never becomes a
  different person.
- The prop stays the same object: same colour, same counted features, same total length, held
  the same way, and it stays attached to the hand rather than floating free.
- No extra limbs, no third hand, no duplicate of the character, no second prop.
- It does not walk, jump, crouch to the floor, turn its back or leave the frame; the feet stay
  on the ground, near the same spot, and the weight may only shift.
- Lighting, background colour and shadow never flicker, drift or change brightness between
  cells.

FAILURE
(a) A sheet in which the character holds the same pose and the prop the same angle in all 16
    cells is a FAILED sheet.
(b) A sheet in which the face, costume, prop or proportions differ from cell to cell is also a
    FAILED sheet.

OUTPUT
- 4 columns, 4 rows, equal cells, no visible grid lines and no gaps between cells.
- No borders, frames, panels, text, numbers, captions, watermarks or logos.
- Each cell is a finished frame of the same single shot; the whole figure and the whole effect
  fit inside their cell with a small even margin and are not cut off by the cell edge.
