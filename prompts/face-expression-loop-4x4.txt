TASK
Build a 4x4 sprite sheet (16 cells, read left to right, top to bottom) showing ONE single face
performing one continuous run of exaggerated expressions. The sheet is built FROM the supplied
reference photo: the person in that photo is the subject, and every cell must read as the same
person seen by the same camera.
- STYLE: <<< keep the reference's own rendering, named explicitly so it does not drift:
      "flat faceted low-poly portrait, hard edges between facets, no skin texture, no soft
      airbrush shading" / "clean anime cel-shading with a hard outline" / "photographic
      portrait, natural skin" / "16-bit pixel art portrait". State the facet/line/noise size
      stays the same in every cell. >>>
- SUBJECT: <<< one line naming the single person by their fixed, visible features only -
      hair length and parting, face shape, garment and collar. Never a real person's name and
      never a brand. >>>
- ACTION: <<< the expression journey in one sentence, e.g. "his face travels from a neutral
      look into X, snaps wide into Y, bursts into Z, then unwinds back to neutral" >>>

SUBJECT - the one and only face (named before anything else)
- Exactly one person, head and shoulders, cropped at <<< mid-chest / collarbone / chin >>>
  placed in the centre of the frame with a small even margin on all sides. The crop, the head
  size and the position of the face are identical in all 16 cells.
- <<< which way he faces and at what head angle, e.g. "straight on, head level, eyes to
      camera" >>>. The head is never turned, never tilted, never raised and never lowered in
  any cell.

CAMERA - LOCKED
- The camera does not move at all: no orbit, no turn, no zoom, no dolly, no pan, no tilt, no
  roll, no change of framing. Fixed distance, fixed height, fixed crop, fixed background.
- The face never slides sideways, never grows, never shrinks and is never re-framed. Nothing
  in the picture changes except the expression on it.
<<< Note for the operator: this is the strictest lock of all four families. A face reads
translation far more sensitively than a full body does - half a percent of lateral drift is
enough to look like the head is bobbing, so the "never re-framed" line is doing real work here.
Do NOT reuse this file for full-body actions; that is char-action-fixed-4x4.txt. >>>

LOOK
- Background: <<< a flat, seamless, pure black field / the reference's own plain background >>>
  filling the whole cell, identical in all 16 cells. No scenery, no vignette, no glow, no
  texture, no gradient.
- <<< the exact lighting, e.g. "one soft key light from the upper left and one gentle fill from
      the right, as in the reference" >>>. Its direction, intensity and colour temperature
  never change between cells.
- The clothing stays literally the same garment, with the same folds, the same collar and the
  same fastenings in every cell.

THE EXPRESSION ARC - one continuous run, 16 equal samples
- The 16 cells are 16 samples of ONE unbroken performance. No two cells show the same face, no
  expression is held frozen across two cells, and the face never snaps instantly from one
  extreme to another.
- The performance: <<< the journey, as ONE arc. Unlike the motion templates this one is
  allowed to name beats - expressions are discrete states, not a continuous quantity, so the
  hard-rule-5 ban on staged lists does not apply. What still matters is that the joins are
  written as blends, never as a cut. >>> Play it as broad mugging comedy - pushed hard, never
  subtle.
- ANCHORS (milestones, each one is just a single cell you can describe in words):
  - Cell 1: <<< the opening expression, described muscle by muscle - brows, eyelids, mouth
    corners, jaw. Starting and ending on a near-neutral face is what makes the loop seam
    invisible. >>>
  - Cell 5: <<< the first peak, e.g. "the smirk at full strength - the corner of the mouth on
    the viewer's left pulled up high while the other corner pulls slightly down, one eyebrow
    cocked high and the other low, one eye narrowed to a slit, nose wing flared" >>>
  - Cell 9: <<< the second peak. Assign every asymmetric feature an explicit side; "a crooked
    smile" without a side gets mirrored at random from cell to cell. >>>
  - Cell 12: <<< the strongest peak >>>
  - Cell 16: back at the cell-1 expression - this is the loop seam, so that a plain cycle loop
    reads seamless.
- Push the exaggeration to cartoon level: <<< which features carry the exaggeration and how
  far - "brows travel a long way, eyes go from slits to full circles, the mouth opens far
  beyond any natural smile, cheeks and nose wings deform" >>>. Push the shapes hard while the
  person stays themselves.

WHAT MAY CHANGE / WHAT MUST NOT
- MAY change: eyebrows, eyelids, eyes and gaze, nose wings, mouth, cheeks, chin and jaw opening.
- MUST NOT change: the identity, the face structure, the facial proportions, the jaw line, the
  cheekbones, the hair length and hair silhouette, the ear shape, the neck, and every garment.
  No ageing, no beautifying, no skin smoothing, no change of ethnicity, no added glasses,
  beard, hat or makeup, no tears, no sweat drops, no cartoon symbols.
- No second face, no second head, no figure in the background, no shadow of another person.
- No motion lines, no speed lines, no colour flashes, no radial burst behind the head.

FAILURE
(a) A sheet in which the face carries the same expression in all 16 cells is a FAILED sheet.
(b) A sheet in which the person is not recognisably the same person in all 16 cells is also a
    FAILED sheet.

OUTPUT
- 4 columns, 4 rows, equal cells, no visible grid lines and no gaps between cells.
- No borders, frames, panels, captions, text, numbers, watermarks or logos.
- Each cell is a finished frame of the same single shot; the hair and the shoulders fit inside
  their cell with a small even margin and are never cut off by the cell edge.
