TASK
Turn the reference image into a 4x4 sprite sheet (16 cells, read left to right, top to
bottom) describing ONE continuous camera move: a horizontal orbit of exactly 60 degrees
around the scene.

REFERENCE
The reference is a LEGO-style Chinese gate-tower scene on a pure white background: a stone
gate tower with red doors in the middle, flanked by grey tiled-roof buildings, two
minifigures, a large green tree, a golden tree and a horse. Keep every one of these
elements, in the same style, colours and materials, in every cell.

CAMERA
- The camera orbits the scene on a horizontal arc about the scene's vertical centre axis,
  always aimed at the centre of the scene.
- CONSTANT distance, CONSTANT height and CONSTANT focal length. No dolly in or out, no
  zoom, no rise, no drop, no tilt, no roll.
- Cell 1 is the straight-on frontal view (0 degrees).
- Cell 16 is exactly 60 degrees from the start: a three-quarter view that shows the front
  and the right-hand side of the gate tower.
- The 60 degrees is divided into 15 equal steps of 4 degrees, one step per cell.
- The motion is perfectly even. Every step covers the same angle. There is no
  acceleration, no pause, no fast section and no slow section anywhere in the sheet.

WHAT MOVES AND WHAT DOES NOT
- ONLY the camera moves. The scene itself is completely static: it must not rotate, not
  translate, not scale, not change shape.
- This is NOT a turntable spin of the model. Do not rotate the model itself.
- As the camera orbits, near objects slide across the frame faster than far objects. That
  parallax is what sells the 3D orbit.

CONSISTENCY (hard requirements)
- The apparent size of the scene changes by LESS THAN 5% across all 16 cells.
- The ground line and the base of the scene sit at exactly the same height in every cell.
- Identical lighting, identical white balance and identical shadow direction in all 16
  cells. No flicker, no colour drift, no brightness change.
- The same subjects appear in every cell. Never add or remove a building, a tree, a figure
  or the horse, and never move one to a new place in the world.
- Same construction style as the reference: LEGO bricks, clean plastic surfaces, same
  colours.

FRAMING
- Every cell shows the whole scene, complete and uncropped, with consistent framing and
  consistent margins.
- Flat, clean, pure white background around the scene, seamless, with no shadow banding or
  darkening at the cell edges.

OUTPUT
- 4 columns, 4 rows, equal cells, no visible grid lines, no gaps between cells.
- No borders, no frames, no text, no numbers, no watermarks, no captions.
- Each cell is a finished frame of the same single shot, in the same style as the
  reference image.
