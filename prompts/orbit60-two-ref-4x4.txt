TASK
Turn the reference images into a 4x4 sprite sheet (16 cells, read left to right, top to
bottom) describing ONE continuous camera move: a horizontal orbit of exactly 60 degrees
around the building.

TWO REFERENCES - READ BOTH
- Image 1 (exterior) is the MAIN VIEW. It defines what the finished shot looks like: the
  two-storey LEGO-style Chinese building with red-brown timber walls, grey tiled roof, the
  large pink cherry-blossom tree on its right, the small river with a boat and a
  fisher-minifigure at the bottom right, on a bright blue background.
- Image 2 (cutaway) is the STRUCTURE REFERENCE ONLY. It shows the same LEGO-style
  architecture opened up, revealing the interior floors, rooms, furniture, minifigures and
  the grey tiled roofs. Use it only to get the building's real internal structure and
  proportions right. Do NOT draw the cutaway, do NOT open up the walls, do NOT show the
  interior in the final sheet.

REFERENCE (what must appear in every cell)
Keep the exact same building, the same cherry-blossom tree, the same river, the same boat
and minifigures, in the same LEGO construction, the same colours and the same plastic
materials as the references. This is one single static diorama, photographed from
different angles - not a redesigned scene.

CAMERA
- The camera orbits the building on a horizontal arc about its vertical centre axis,
  always aimed at the centre of the building.
- CONSTANT distance, CONSTANT height and CONSTANT focal length. No dolly in or out, no
  zoom, no rise, no drop, no tilt, no roll.
- The camera stays on the FRONT side of the building for the whole move. It never passes
  the side face and never goes behind the building.
- Cell 1 is the straight-on frontal view (0 degrees): we look at the front facade straight
  on, exactly as in Image 1.
- Cell 16 is exactly 60 degrees away from the start: a mild three-quarter view that still
  shows the front facade full and readable, with only a gentle glimpse of the right-hand
  side wall and a little more of the cherry tree.
- The 60 degrees is divided into 15 equal steps of 4 degrees, one step per cell.
- The motion is perfectly even. Every step covers the same angle. There is no
  acceleration, no pause, no fast section and no slow section anywhere in the sheet.

WHAT MOVES AND WHAT DOES NOT
- ONLY the camera moves. The building and everything in the scene is completely static: it
  must not rotate, not translate, not scale, not change shape, not open up.
- This is NOT a turntable spin of the model. Do not rotate the model itself.
- As the camera orbits, the near cherry tree and river slide across the frame faster than
  the far wall behind. That parallax is what sells the 3D orbit.

CONSISTENCY (hard requirements)
- The apparent size of the building changes by LESS THAN 5% across all 16 cells.
- The base of the building and the ground line sit at exactly the same height in every
  cell.
- Identical lighting, identical white balance and identical shadow direction in all 16
  cells. No flicker, no colour drift, no brightness change.
- The same subjects appear in every cell. Never add or remove a building, a tree, a boat,
  a lantern or a minifigure, and never move one to a new place in the world.
- Same construction style as the references: LEGO bricks, clean plastic surfaces, same
  colours.

FRAMING
- Every cell shows the whole diorama, complete and uncropped, with consistent framing and
  consistent margins. The building never touches the edge of a cell.
- Flat, clean, evenly lit bright-blue background around the diorama, matching the
  reference background, seamless, with no banding and no darkening at the cell edges.

OUTPUT
- 4 columns, 4 rows, equal cells, no visible grid lines, no gaps between cells.
- No borders, no frames, no text, no numbers, no watermarks, no captions.
- Each cell is a finished frame of the same single shot, in the same style as the
  reference images.
